## Before anything

0. Export your current world to `.json` from the browser you'll test in. The finals share that browser's storage with whatever Cartulary last ran there, so the first open loads what it finds.

## Chrome or Edge, image folder connected

1. Open `Cartulary_v1.7.html`. Your world appears as you left it, with no error.
2. Open every view once from the sidebar: Home, Project, All entries, an entry list and an editor, the Template Builder, Fabula, Stemma, Cartographia, Concordance, Timeline; Vertical, Timeline; Chronometric, Kalendarium, Ephemeris, Glossopoeia, Linea. Nothing throws.
3. Fabula: + New Story. On People, add a cast member linked to a Character. On Outline, + Part / Act, + Chapter, + Scene, and + Beat; the scene shows its position (I.1.A). Timeline draws the scene in Chrono, Narratio, and Anachronia. Reload: all of it is there.
4. On the story's page, Export ↓ as HTML; the story bible opens.
5. Stemma: + New Rota, add two children and link one to an entry, then Undo once. + New Tabula: a note, a link card, a column, and a line drawn between two cards; move a card and the line's end follows. Reload: both are as you left them.
6. Edit an entry and reload within a second or two: the edit is there.
7. Settings: Browser storage says what the browser answered. Back up now, then look in the image folder: `cartulary-backups` holds a `.json` named for the world and the time.
8. Rename an entry another entry `[[links]]` to: the window asks; Rename and update links. The linking entry's link shows the new name, and the renamed entry's page ends with Referenced by, naming the linking entry.
9. Delete an entry something links to: the window counts what it will clear. Cancel.
10. All entries: Filter on a field, then Save list; the list appears under Lists in the sidebar and opens filtered.
11. On a template's entry list, Export ↓, then Export entries… as CSV. Import entries… that file: the mapping screen's preview counts the entries as updates, and nothing is emptied.
12. In the Template Builder, give a Grid field a starting shape. A new entry of that template opens with the grid's rows and columns.
13. On a map in the editor, end a line on a pin. Drag the pin: the line's end follows. Save, and the map's view shows the same.
14. Export the world as HTML: the footer reads "Generated by Cartulary v1.7".
