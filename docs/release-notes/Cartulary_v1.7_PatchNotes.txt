CARTULARY v1.7 - PATCH NOTES
============================

v1.7 is the Letters and Pipes update. Letters is two new ways to plan:
Fabula, a view for the stories set in your world, and a second Stemma
layout, the tabula, a board for loose thinking. Pipes is the plumbing
underneath everything: saving you can count on, backups, links that
survive deletes and renames, a real filter on the entry list, and a way
to move a template's entries in and out as JSON or CSV.


FABULA
------

A view for planning the stories set in your world, with the writing
itself done somewhere else. Fabula's records aren't entries: they live in
their own part of the save file and point out at your entries.

  - The index lists every corpus with its stories in reading order, then
    the stories in no corpus. A corpus gathers stories that belong
    together: a series, a shared setting, an anthology.
  - A story's page has seven tabs: Story (the premise and folding
    panels for the rest), People (the cast, their arcs, and their
    relationships), Plot (threads, plot points under the structure's
    slots, and promises and payoffs, with unpaid ones flagged),
    Outline, Meaning (themes and motifs, and where each runs), Notes
    (research notes and what they're used in), and Timeline.
  - Outline is the story as one tree: acts, chapters, scenes, and each
    scene's beats, added in place by title. Positions follow where
    records sit (acts I, II, III; chapters 1, 2, 3; scenes A, B, C;
    beats i, ii, iii), and show on every page and reference, "II.4.A".
    Rows move by their arrows, by Alt and an arrow, or by drag; the
    plain arrows walk the tree from the keyboard.
  - The timeline draws a story's scenes, or a corpus's, three ways:
    Chrono (story time), Narratio (telling order), and Anachronia (the
    two together, so flashbacks and leaps show).
  - Fabula's eleven record kinds each have a schema the template builder
    edits. Fields Fabula reads stay put; everything else is yours.
  - The story bible exports a story or a corpus as HTML or Markdown,
    with its own include-secrets choice.
  - Plot Thread and Theme / Motif are Fabula's now: still entries,
    listed and edited from Fabula's index.
  - A story's Brainstorm field links the stemmas behind it, rota or
    tabula, each opening on its canvas.


STEMMA v2
---------

Stemma has two layouts now.

  - The rota is the radial tree it was, rebuilt: a focal node with its
    children in slots around it, drilled into by a click. It gains
    undo, reordering by drag and by Alt and the arrows, a full keyboard
    walk, zoom, and linked nodes that can point at any record in the
    world.
  - The tabula is new: a board without edges. Five kinds of card (Note,
    Link, Image, Column, and Board, a board inside the board), columns
    with lanes and rows, stacks under cards, a multi-selection, and a
    drawing layer of lines whose ends stick to the cards they land on.
  - Both export as Markdown for reading, beside the .stemma.json that
    comes back in.


SAVING AND BACKUPS
------------------

  - A change is written 250 ms after you stop, a Save writes at once,
    and whatever is waiting is written when the page is hidden.
  - A save that fails says so in a Not saved. banner, with Try now and
    Export now, and keeps trying until one lands.
  - Cartulary asks the browser to keep its storage, and Settings says
    what the browser answered.
  - Folder backups: with an image folder connected, the world is written
    into a cartulary-backups folder inside it as you edit, at most every
    10 minutes, keeping the newest 10. A backup counts for the backup
    reminder. A folder whose permission lapses is kept, with Reconnect.
  - A reset or an import keeps your restore points.
  - A world holding two records with one id loads repaired instead of
    refusing to open.


LINKS
-----

  - Referenced by: at the end of an entry's, a map's, a body's, a
    corpus's, and a Fabula record's page, every record that links there,
    by the fields it links through.
  - Every delete of a record something links to asks first, in one
    window that counts what it will clear, and then clears it.
  - A [[link]] reaches any record by name: an entry, a body, a map, a
    stemma, a Fabula record, or a language. The exports resolve links
    the way the page does.
  - Renaming a record that [[links]] reach by its old name asks first,
    and can update every such link (after a Before rename restore point)
    or add the old name to its Former Names. It also warns when the new
    name already belongs to another record.
  - Concordance draws its edges from the same index, so links it used to
    miss now show.


THE ENTRY LIST
--------------

  - All entries lists every entry in the world, the way to search all of
    it at once.
  - Filter: conditions on any field, by its label across templates, with
    operators for its type, joined by All or Any, shown as chips.
  - Saved lists: a filter kept by name in the sidebar's Lists section,
    travelling with the world.
  - The search reads what the page shows: a reference by its record's
    name, a date as your calendar writes it, a Long Text without its
    markup.


A TEMPLATE'S ENTRIES, IN AND OUT
--------------------------------

  - Export entries... writes a template's entries as JSON, in the world
    file's own shapes, or as a CSV for a spreadsheet.
  - Import entries... reads either back through one mapping screen: the
    file's fields matched to yours, a preview counting what it adds,
    updates, leaves, and skips, and a restore point before it writes. An
    import never empties a field.
  - A CSV from elsewhere reads too: dates as your calendar writes them or
    as numbers, own values beside a dropdown's options, values with ";"
    escaped as \;, and UTF-8, Windows-1252, or UTF-16 files.


MAPS
----

  - A line's end put down on a pin or a stamp sticks to it and follows
    it, in the editor, the view, and the downloads.
  - A long line keeps handles at its two ends.


FIELDS AND TEMPLATES
--------------------

  - A Grid field can take a starting shape in the template builder:
    rows, columns, and a cell type per column, which every new entry's
    grid starts as. An empty grid with no shape asks for rows by
    columns.
  - A multi-select can take values of your own under Other.
  - A dependent dropdown keeps its Other text under any choice of its
    parent.
  - References can point at records that aren't entries (a body, a
    language, a map, a stemma) and show them by name.
  - The default calendar is Common Year.


SMALLER CHANGES
---------------

- Chronometric's lanes make room for what's drawn, long titles wrap, era
  names stay clear of the year labels, and the chart fits its frame.
- Fields keep what you typed: a Date reads its own day in any time zone,
  and a Required Number field saves.
- The in-app help covers everything above, with a new topic, Import &
  Export Entries, and the save file's schema in full. cartulary.world
  has pages for Fabula and Stemma, and the rest written from the help.


UNDER THE HOOD
--------------

Cartulary is still one HTML file. The module tree it's built from is 62
files now, with Fabula and Stemma each a folder of their own, and every
change still has to parse, pass its own checks, and reassemble against a
frozen reference before it ships. The Readable build ships beside the
compiled one, with the JSX source intact.
